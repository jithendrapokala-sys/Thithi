About the Team

Meet Jithendra Pokala

Founder & CEO, Thithi.in

Where Ancient Wisdom Meets Modern Business

I have always believed that there is more to a decision than what we can see on a spreadsheet. Business is built on decisions: When should we act? Who should we work with? Which direction should we take? When should we begin?

A Journey Rooted in Dharma

Our ancestral lineage connects with Pokala Dammakka, a devotee associated with the sacred tradition of Bhadrachalam. My grandfather, Late Pokala Dhananjaya Rao Naidu garu (Atreya Gotra), served as Tahsildar and Deputy Collector in the Revenue Department (involved in the Tirumala second ghat road development) and on deputation as Executive Officer at Sri Veera Venkata Satyanarayana Swamy Vari Devasthanam, Annavaram (1966–68). Their service instilled one principle: Knowledge should ultimately become service.

My Spiritual Journey

My inclination towards Sanatana Dharma began at age 13. Over the past 25 years, I studied the Ramayana, Mahabharata, Bhagavatam, Chanakya Neeti, Vidura Neeti, Artha Shastra, Jyotisha Shastra, Prashna Shastra, and KP Astrology to address one core question: Can ancient knowledge help a person make better decisions in the present?
