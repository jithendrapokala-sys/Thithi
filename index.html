About the Team

Meet Jithendra Pokala

Founder & CEO, Thithi.in

Where Ancient Wisdom Meets Modern Business

I have always believed that there is more to a decision than what we can see on a spreadsheet. Business is built on decisions: When should we act? Who should we work with? Which direction should we take? When should we begin?

A Journey Rooted in Dharma & Public Service

My lineage connects with Pokala Dammakka, a devotee associated with the sacred tradition of Bhadrachalam. My grandfather, Late Pokala Dhananjaya Rao Naidu garu (Atreya Gotra), served as Tahsildar and Deputy Collector in the Revenue Department (involved in the Tirumala second ghat road development) and served as Executive Officer at Sri Veera Venkata Satyanarayana Swamy Vari Devasthanam, Annavaram (1966–68). These legacies taught me one principle: Knowledge should ultimately become service.

Spiritual Grounding & The Science of Timing

My inclination towards Sanatana Dharma began at age 13. Over 25 years, I studied sacred literature—including the Ramayana, Mahabharata, Bhagavatam, Chanakya Neeti, Artha Shastra, Jyotisha, Prashna Shastra, and KP Astrology. My pursuit was practical: Can ancient knowledge help a leader make better decisions in the present?

The KP & Placidus House Synthesis

Krishnamurti Paddhati (KP) uses Sub-Lords to divide nakshatras with mathematical precision, combined with the house system formalized by Italian astronomer Placidus de Titis (University of Padua, est. 1222). This convergence of ancient Indian stellar astrology and European mathematical calculation proves that ancient knowledge can be preserved while its application evolves.
